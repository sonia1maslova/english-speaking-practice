# English Speaking Practice

This repository records the English words and phrases I want to use in real conversations, especially online speaking lessons with foreign teachers.

## Purpose

When I know what I want to say but cannot express it naturally in English, I save the word or phrase here after looking it up. The goal is to review useful expressions in context and gradually turn passive vocabulary into speaking ability.

## Structure

- [`vocabulary/`](vocabulary/) — words and phrases collected during daily practice.
- [`speaking-sessions/`](speaking-sessions/) — notes from speaking lessons and conversations.
- [`templates/`](templates/) — reusable templates for new vocabulary and session notes.

Start with [`vocabulary/word-log.md`](vocabulary/word-log.md). Add one row whenever you look up an expression you wanted to use.

## Recommended entry

For each item, record:

- the idea you wanted to express;
- the natural English word or phrase;
- pronunciation or stress when useful;
- register and common collocations;
- one example sentence related to your own situation;
- the source or date you checked it;
- whether you can use it without looking it up.

## AI-assisted notes

Some explanations, examples, and translations may be AI-assisted. They should be checked against a dictionary or another reliable source, especially when nuance, register, idioms, or regional usage matters.

Do not put private lesson details, names, contact information, or other sensitive personal information in this public repository.
